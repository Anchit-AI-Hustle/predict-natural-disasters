Data sources: GDACS, USGS, EMSC, NASA EONET, NOAA NHC, NOAA NWS, ReliefWeb, USGS Volcanoes, Open-Meteo, NASA POWER
  • GDACS
    Alerts for earthquakes, tropical cyclones, floods, droughts, wildfires and volcanoes worldwide, with impact figures.
  • USGS Earthquake Hazards Program
    Every earthquake of magnitude 2.5 or more in the past 7 days, and the shocks the 7-day aftershock outlooks follow.
  • EMSC
    An independent check on earthquakes of magnitude 4.5 or more.
  • NASA EONET
    Wildfires, storms, volcanoes and other events seen from satellites.
  • NOAA National Hurricane Center
    Active tropical cyclones in the Atlantic and eastern and central Pacific only.
  • NOAA National Weather Service
    Severe and extreme weather warnings in the United States.
  • ReliefWeb (UN OCHA)
    Humanitarian disaster declarations, by country.
  • USGS Volcano Hazards Program
    US-monitored volcanoes at a raised alert level.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding
    Places ReliefWeb’s country-level records, which carry no coordinates, at their country.
  • Open-Meteo Forecast API
    The coming week’s temperature, rain, dew point and wind that the heat, rain and fire-weather forecasts start from.
  • NASA POWER
    Each place’s 30-year record (1996 to 2025) a forecast is judged against, and the measured days it is graded on.
