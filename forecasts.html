How we make forecasts

Methodology →
1. Spot

We look for unusual weather, water or ground conditions.

2. Where and when

We mark the area that may be hit and the dates the forecast covers.

3. Keep them apart

We never show an official report as our forecast, or our forecast as an official report.

4. Check

Later, we check each forecast against what really happened. We lock the rules for that check when we make the forecast: the dates, the area, the cause and what counts as damage.
