Forecast mechanism

1. Trigger

Atmospheric, hydrological, seismic or geophysical conditions that could initiate the event.

2. Transmission

How the trigger propagates into flooding, slope failure, shaking, fire spread or other hazard processes.

3. Exposure

Communities, transport, drainage, terrain and infrastructure intersecting the hazard footprint.

4. Verification

The forecast is graded only against pre-defined geography, timing and impact criteria. Rainfall or a warning alone does not prove a flood forecast correct.
