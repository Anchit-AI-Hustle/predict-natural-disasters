What counts as evidence?

Observed impacts

Documented flooding, inundation, landslides, evacuations, rescues, infrastructure disruption, damage or casualties.

Official sources

National meteorological and disaster agencies, authoritative emergency bulletins and verified incident reporting.

What does not count

Heavy rain by itself, a warning by itself, or ordinary seasonal conditions without meaningful disaster impacts.
