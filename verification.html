Verification standard

Timing
Observed impact must occur inside the forecast window.
Geography
Observed impact must overlap the frozen target geography.
Mechanism
The forecasted hazard mechanism must actually occur.
Meaningful impact
A pre-existing warning or signal alone does not count as a hit. Impacts must be independently observed and materially relevant.
Outcome
Final grading is HIT / PARTIAL HIT / MISS / UNVERIFIABLE with source evidence retained.
