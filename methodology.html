How the system works

Methodology

The platform combines authoritative observations, official warnings, geographic relevance, forecast signals and post-event verification. Forecasts are designed to be falsifiable and auditable rather than presented as certainty.

1 · Collect

Ingest current events and warnings from sources such as USGS, NASA EONET, meteorological agencies and disaster authorities.

2 · Normalize

Map each event to hazard type, time, coordinates, country, state/province, city, source class and freshness.

3 · Rank

Prioritize events using severity, urgency, source confidence, freshness, exposure and geographic relevance.

4 · Forecast

Publish bounded outlooks with explicit geography, date window, hazard chain and success criteria.

5 · Freeze

Lock prediction wording before the outcome so location, date and criteria cannot be moved afterward.

6 · Verify

Compare actual impacts with the frozen record and assign HIT, PARTIAL HIT or MISS only after evidence review.

Probability policy

No fabricated percentages

The interface does not show “92% probability” unless a validated calibrated probability model supports that number.

Risk Index instead

Where appropriate, the system uses a 0–100 Risk Index or categorical levels such as Critical, High, Moderate and Low.

Earthquake limitation

The platform does not claim scientifically established exact date/time/location prediction for earthquakes.
