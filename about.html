Core principles

Useful over sensational

Hazard information should help users understand risk without overstating certainty.

Transparent sources

Observed data and official warnings should show where they came from and how fresh they are.

Accountable forecasts

Forecasts should be frozen before outcomes and judged afterward against explicit criteria.

Live Map

Interactive global monitoring of current natural hazards with location search and hazard filtering.

Forecasts

Forward-looking high-risk outlooks with timing, geography and hazard type clearly displayed.

Official Alerts

A separate warning center that keeps government and scientific alerts distinct from platform forecasts.

Regions

World-to-city views that prioritize hazards relevant to the geography the user selected.

Verification

Evidence-based grading of forecasts after their frozen verification window closes.

Insights

Cross-hazard context and trends presented as analysis, not deterministic future claims.

This platform provides natural-hazard information for awareness and preparedness. It is not a substitute for official emergency warnings, evacuation orders or instructions from local authorities.
