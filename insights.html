Flood & landslide coupling

Sustained or very intense rainfall can create compound risk: rivers rise, drainage fails, slopes saturate, and transport disruption can follow. The platform treats these chains as related but verifies each impact separately.

Geographic clustering

Hazard relevance changes sharply by location. A globally important event may be irrelevant to a city-level primary feed, which is why geographic relevance receives strong ranking weight.

Forecast discipline

Forecast value is measured through later verification. A warning or rainfall signal alone is not treated as success unless the frozen outcome criteria are met.

Interpretation guide

Trend ≠ deterministic prediction

Historical and current patterns can inform risk, but they do not create certainty about exact future events.

Risk changes with time

Fresh observations and official warnings can materially alter ranking and local relevance.

Compound hazards matter

Flood, landslide, storm and infrastructure impacts may occur in sequence and should be assessed together.
