Geographic relevance rules

City first

Exact-city and nearby district hazards rank above unrelated national or international events.

State / province inheritance

Relevant state and national warnings can appear when they materially affect the selected locality.

Global context separated

Major international events may appear in a distinct context section, never as local primary hazards.
