Coverage is global on Earth and includes all countries and territories recognized by the browser's internationalization dataset. There is no authoritative live natural-disaster reporting network for every extraterrestrial body in the universe, so the product does not fabricate non-Earth coverage. If a source is temporarily unavailable, verified fallback records are labelled as such.

Historical Timeline

Earliest documented records in this archive → present
No scientifically complete list of every natural disaster since Earth began exists. This archive starts with early disasters that have credible archaeological, geological or written evidence, then becomes progressively more complete as modern instrumental records begin. It must not be read as “the first natural disaster ever.”
All timestamps default to IST in 12-hour AM/PM format. Use the timezone selector in the top navigation to view the same source timestamps in another timezone.
