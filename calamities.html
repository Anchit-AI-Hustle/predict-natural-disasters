Timeline of past disasters

From the earliest recorded disasters to today
No complete list of every natural disaster in Earth’s history exists. This timeline starts with early disasters that have good archaeological, geological or written evidence. It becomes more complete from the time modern instruments began recording. Its first entry is not “the first natural disaster ever”.
Times are shown in IST by default. To see them in another time zone, use the time zone menu at the top of the page.
