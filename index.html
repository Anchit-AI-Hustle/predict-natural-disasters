How verification works

Rain alone does not count as a successful forecast. Final grading requires meaningful impacts that match the frozen location, timing and mechanism.

Methodology

Forecasts are issued with a fixed geography, target window, hazard mechanism and verification criteria. Once issued, those criteria are frozen so the result cannot be retrofitted after the event.

  • Official observations and alerts are kept separate from forecasts.
  • Risk scores are indices, not calibrated probabilities.
  • Earthquake timing is not presented as deterministic prediction.

Insights

Compare active hazards, forecast windows, observed events and verification outcomes across regions. Current live feeds use USGS earthquake data and NASA EONET open events.

  • Geography-aware event ranking
  • Hazard and recency filtering
  • Forecast-versus-outcome tracking

About

Predict Natural Disasters is a global hazard intelligence and forecast-verification dashboard designed to make signals understandable, traceable and accountable.

For emergency decisions, always follow local authorities and official warning agencies.
