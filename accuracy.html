Forecast verification

A forecast counts as a prediction only if it was frozen before the hazard it names was observed. An outlook about a flood already under way, an aftershock outlook following an earthquake, and a benchmark written after its own window opened are all real forecasts, and none is a prediction of something nobody knew about. The two are reported separately below and are never added together.

Observed disasters

Every significant observed disaster on record, including the ones nothing forecast. Where no scan covering that hazard and moment is recorded, the reason cannot be established either way, and it says so rather than being attributed to coverage or to forecasting.

DisasterHazardWhereOnsetDeathsDisplacedEvidencePredicted?Why not

Precision answers “when the system predicted an event, how often was it right?” Recall answers “of significant disasters that actually occurred, how many had a qualifying frozen prediction beforehand?” Pending forecasts are excluded until their target window matures. Signal-derived outlooks are not credited merely because the source warning already existed.
