Independent prediction record

A forecast can only count as a prediction if it was frozen before the hazard it names was observed. An outlook written about a flood already under way, an aftershock outlook following an earthquake, and a benchmark written after its own window opened are all real forecasts, and none of them is a prediction of something nobody knew about. They are graded here under their own headings instead of being added together.
