Audit rules

The geography, date window and success criteria are frozen. They cannot be widened after the outcome.

A source warning, heavy rainfall or model signal is evidence of mechanism/conditions, not automatically proof that the predicted disaster occurred.

Final grading must cite authoritative observed impact evidence.
